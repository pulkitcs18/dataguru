How it works

You write. We scope. We deliver.

DataGuru does not take orders through an account on this site. A request starts in your own email client and stays there.

  1. Write

    Use the request form or the plain email link on the contact page. Include your name, a work email, your company, the sports, and what the data needs to support — a broadcast graphic, a newsroom, an operations desk, a sportsbook, an app, or an internal model. A rough note is enough. You do not create an account.

  2. Scope

    We reply with a written scope: sports and competitions, fields, history window, format, cadence, and anything we cannot include. You can narrow or widen the list before delivery starts. If a competition is outside what we can supply, we say so.

  3. Deliver

    We provide the data on the agreed path. Questions, corrections, and new competitions go back through email. Stopping a delivery is an email, not a setting in a dashboard. There is no dashboard on this site.

What we need in the first note

  • The sports, and the competitions if you already know them
  • The job the data will do
  • How fresh the file needs to be
  • A work email we can reply to

What this website does not do

  • Sign you in
  • Take payment
  • Show proprietary live scores
  • Treat sample layouts as current results

The transfer path is part of the scope. JSON and CSV files are the usual agreement. Timing is confirmed in that same note. This page does not publish a response-time promise.
