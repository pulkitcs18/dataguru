What DataGuru does

Five families of data, requested rather than browsed.

Each family is something a client can ask for. The descriptions are the offer. They are not a connection you can open from this page.

  1. Scores

    Outcomes and match status, including set and game splits for tennis, and period or inning splits when another sport uses them and your scope includes them.

  2. Fixtures

    The schedule around those scores: who plays, in which competition, and when the event is set to start.

  3. Live events

    Ordered in-play actions — aces, games, tiebreaks, goals, and the equivalents in other sports — delivered on a cadence we agree in writing.

  4. Player and team reference

    Names, roles, and stable identifiers so your systems can join one file to the next.

  5. Historical results

    Completed matches for a window you name, for backfill, research, and context.

How it works

Email, scope, delivery.

There is no account to open. A request starts in your own email client and stays there.

  1. Write

    Send the sports, your company, and the job the data needs to do.

  2. Scope

    We reply with competitions, fields, format, cadence, and anything we cannot include.

  3. Deliver

    You receive the agreed data. Changes go back through email.
