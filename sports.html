Sports coverage

Sports we will scope with you.

Football, tennis, American football, and ice hockey are the flagship set. Basketball, baseball, cricket, rugby, golf, motorsport, and other major sports can be included when we confirm them for your request.

Football

Soccer

Club and national-team football. Ask for scores, fixtures, and in-match events for the competitions you name — domestic leagues, cups, or international windows. The scope we send back is the coverage. It is not a rights claim over the sport.

  • Match scores and status
  • Fixtures and kickoff context
  • Goals, cards, and substitutions where those events are in scope
  • Team and player reference for the competitions you select

Tennis

Match and draw

Match winners, set and game scores where you need them, order of play, and player reference. Suitable for a tour, a team event, or a list of tournaments you write down.

  • Match scores and status
  • Set progression when it is part of the scope
  • Order of play and withdrawals you ask us to carry
  • Player reference for the events you include

American football

Game record

Game scores, schedules, and play-level events when the desk needs them, plus team and player reference. Professional and collegiate competitions are both in bounds to discuss. DataGuru is not presented as a league feed.

  • Game scores by period
  • Fixtures and kickoff context
  • Play-level events where the scope includes them
  • Team and player reference

Ice hockey

Period and roster

Period and final scores, fixtures, and roster reference for the leagues you specify. Goal and penalty sequences can sit inside a live-event scope.

  • Team and player reference
